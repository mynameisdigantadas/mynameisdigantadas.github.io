1. Introduction

Welcome to DIGANTA PROFILE. This Payments and Refund Policy describes the general rules and procedures that may apply when users purchase products, access premium features, subscribe to services, or make other payments through our website.

We aim to maintain a transparent and understandable payment environment. We want customers to understand the amount they are paying, the service they are purchasing, the applicable conditions, and the circumstances under which a refund may be available.

This policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, and any product-specific terms displayed during checkout.

References to "we", "us", "our", or "DIGANTA PROFILE" refer to the website operator. References to "you", "your", or "customer" refer to a visitor, buyer, subscriber, or user of our services.

2. Acceptance of This Policy

By making a payment through our website, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to review the relevant payment terms.

You are responsible for checking the product description, price, quantity, billing period, and any special conditions before completing your transaction.

Important:

If you do not agree with the applicable payment conditions, do not proceed with the transaction. Contact our support team if you need clarification before purchasing.

6. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed through third-party payment gateways, banks, financial institutions, or authorized payment service providers.

When you initiate a transaction, your payment may pass through multiple verification and authorization stages.

6.1 Authorization

The payment provider may check whether your selected payment method is valid and whether sufficient funds or available credit exist.

6.2 Verification

Certain transactions may require additional verification, such as a UPI approval, bank authentication, OTP, or other security procedure.

6.3 Transaction Completion

A transaction will generally be considered completed only when the relevant payment provider confirms successful processing and the order is recorded appropriately in our system.

7. Payment Confirmation and Receipts

After a successful payment, a confirmation message, transaction reference, receipt, or order confirmation may be provided through the available communication channel.

Customers should preserve the following information:

  • Order ID.
  • Transaction ID.
  • Payment reference number.
  • Payment date and time.
  • Amount paid.
  • Relevant confirmation message.

These details may be required when requesting support, investigating a transaction, or submitting a refund request.

8. Failed, Pending, or Incomplete Transactions

Sometimes a payment may fail, remain pending, or show an uncertain status because of network interruptions, banking delays, payment provider issues, or technical problems.

8.1 Failed Payments

If a payment fails, the order may not be completed. Please check your payment application or bank statement before attempting another transaction.

8.2 Pending Payments

A pending transaction may require additional time for confirmation from the payment provider.

8.3 Amount Debited but Order Not Confirmed

If money has been debited but the order remains unconfirmed, contact our support team with the transaction reference and supporting details.

Do not immediately make repeated payments.

First verify the transaction status with your bank or payment application to avoid accidental duplicate transactions.

9. Duplicate Payments

A duplicate payment may occur when the same order is accidentally paid for more than once.

If you believe that you have made a duplicate payment, contact us with:

  • The relevant order ID.
  • Both transaction reference numbers.
  • The transaction dates.
  • The amounts charged.
  • Relevant payment confirmation records.

We will review the available information and coordinate with the relevant payment provider where necessary.

If a duplicate charge is confirmed, any eligible refund will be processed through an appropriate method, subject to applicable law and payment provider rules.

10. General Refund Policy

Refunds are reviewed according to the nature of the purchased product or service, the transaction status, the delivery status, the reason for the request, applicable checkout terms, and relevant legal requirements.

We aim to handle legitimate refund requests fairly, consistently, and within a reasonable period.

Important refund condition:

A payment is not automatically refundable simply because a customer changes their mind after receiving or using a product or service. However, statutory consumer rights and any specific refund promise displayed during purchase will be respected.

10.1 Refund Review

A refund request may be reviewed using transaction records, order details, communication history, product delivery information, and other relevant evidence.

10.2 Partial Refunds

Where appropriate and legally permitted, a partial refund may be considered if only part of an order was delivered, fulfilled, or usable.

10.3 Full Refunds

A full refund may be considered where the transaction qualifies under the applicable refund conditions, including cases involving confirmed duplicate payments or a service that we failed to provide as agreed.

11. Refund Eligibility

Subject to applicable law and the specific purchase terms, a refund may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • A confirmed duplicate payment.
  • A payment successfully collected without a corresponding order.
  • An order cancelled by us before fulfillment.
  • A paid service that we are unable to provide as agreed.
  • A material technical failure that prevents access to a purchased service and cannot reasonably be resolved.
  • A transaction processed in error and confirmed through investigation.
  • Another circumstance in which a refund is required by law or promised under the applicable product terms.

11.1 Evidence Requirements

We may request reasonable supporting evidence to verify the transaction and understand the issue.

11.2 Individual Assessment

Refund eligibility may depend on the specific facts of each transaction. Similar requests may receive different outcomes if their circumstances differ.

12. Non-Refundable Situations

To the extent permitted by applicable law and the purchase-specific terms, certain circumstances may not qualify for a refund.

Examples may include:

  • A customer changes their mind after successfully receiving a service that was correctly provided.
  • A customer fails to review the product description before purchasing.
  • A customer provides incorrect information that prevents successful delivery.
  • A customer loses access because they violate applicable usage rules.
  • A customer requests a refund for an already completed service without a valid refund basis.
  • A customer requests compensation for issues caused by an unrelated third-party system, where we are not legally responsible.
Legal rights are not excluded.

Nothing in this section removes any refund, cancellation, or consumer protection rights that apply under mandatory law.
